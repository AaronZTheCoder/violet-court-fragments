# The Violet Court Fragments

## Narrative Atlas

For the Host. This atlas connects the existing setting, stories, and rules into one continuing chronicle. It is a reference, not an additional adventure. Use it with [Campaign Continuity](CAMPAIGN_CONTINUITY.md), the [Host's Codex](HOST_CODEX.md), the [setting book](GAME_DESIGN.md), and the [Core Rulebook](CORE_RULEBOOK.md).

A node is a situation that can change through play. Its parent is a condition that brings it into the story. A person, resource, record, or obligation can carry its result into another scene. The party may refuse, solve, revisit, or take an unlisted route. Record what happens without forcing the next node.

### The Central Question

The empire survives through land, labor, food, transport, Qi, credit, shelter, law, and shared accounts. The same network can protect people and decide whose losses count. The Crimson Dusk damages places within that network. Surviving records describe injuries from the founding and the later sealing. Whether those events caused the Dusk, and what the Dusk truly is, remain open questions.

Who may be seen, receive help, and shape promises made in their name? A family seeking grain, a worker seeking safe wages, a survivor protecting a name, and an officiant considering consent can confront this question without sharing one secret or serving one cause.

### Schematic Map

The directions and routes below are established, but the diagram is not to scale and does not fix all borders.

Northern Expanse → timber, reagents, watershed knowledge → Xiaoyuan

Eastern Foundries → machinery, work crews, railway → Xiaoyuan

Southern Granary → grain, silk, freight, railway → Xiaoyuan

Xiaoyuan → restricted railway → Western Bulwark

Western Bulwark → escorts and local routes → Iron Gate and frontier passes

Western Grasslands and Amber Coast → displaced people and claims → reception points and Xiaoyuan

Eastern Valleys → Nine Willow Bend, water and testimony → petitions and sanctuary

Salt Steppe → herding routes and remembered service → provincial accounts

Xiaoyuan → allocations, credit, law, recitation → reachable offices and working relays across the Domains

The Jade River and Imperial Highway meet at Silver Ford. Harmony Rest inspects traffic approaching the capital. Iron Gate has a public lantern relay. The locations of every river branch and the Great Weir are not fixed; a shared river system does not prove a direct route between two places.

Three established rail lines connect Xiaoyuan with the Eastern Foundries, Southern Granary, and Western Bulwark. A railway terminus at Iron Gate is not established. Travel to the Foundries by rail takes about two days; an ordinary courier message takes about three. A courier between Xiaoyuan and Gate's End takes about two weeks each way. Public lantern relays carry scheduled broadcasts and short reports; damage can interrupt coverage. Paired Qi Resonance Towers carry immediate confidential messages at exceptional cost. Establish capacity, authentication, and travel time before a decision depends on them.

### Narrative Tree

The arrows show possible connections, not a required order. Each node lists its parent systems, how play might reach it, the choices it offers, and a result that can carry forward.

#### Opening

**R0. The living empire.** Parents: S1 to S8. A person or place needs something the network can supply. The party chooses what matters and what it will undertake. The people involved continue acting even if the party leaves.

**A0. A delivery or another beginning.** Parents: R0, S2, S3, S8. Reed Basket offers the Twenty Seventh Relief Docket, or the party meets someone affected by the same situation. Deliver, amend, negotiate, refuse, or find another route. The two copies, missing household, and receiving office can return.

**A1. The storehouse.** Parents: A0, S2, S4, S5. Grain is present but the depot lacks a distribution order. Seek a signature, invoke an existing emergency authority, obtain other supplies, accept the risk of unauthorized issue, or leave. Keep the porter, attendant, grain, and actual order.

**A2. The witness and reserve.** Parents: A0 or A1, S2, S3, S4, S7. Lin seeks protection for Tao Chen. Offer shelter, escort, representation, or another arrangement; consult ledgers independently. Chen's payment copies and Zhang's reserve evidence are distinct sources. Missing refined Qi affects allocations; it does not power the Audit or establish the Dusk's cause.

**A3. The Audit.** Parents: A2 or an actual summons, S3, S4, S6, S8. Meng examines available accounts and allegations. Testify to known facts, challenge a statement, seek protection or representation, or decline attendance with known consequences. Carry forward accepted evidence, the finding, and the people involved. Apply the Risk baseline once.

**A4. The shared account.** Parents: A0 or A1, S3, S5. A routing slip or depot ledger connects relief to the scheduled rite. Verify the annex, seek a separate account or stay, or let the terms proceed. The release order and provisional roll remain separate records.

**A5. The rider and niche.** Parents: A4, S4, S5, S7. An admission rider proposes future Court authority over Temple niches. Amend, accept, challenge, or leave it. Incense Crown and chosen representatives decide for themselves. Keep the actual wording, witnesses, and rite outcome.

**A6. An opening with a result.** Parents: any of A1 to A5. Resolve the relief, roll, and consent questions as far as actions allow. Record what officials and affected people do when the party does not intervene. Return to someone living with the result. A wider mystery does not erase delivered supplies, an accepted claim, or withheld consent.

#### Work, Water, and Land

**W1. Crew terms.** Parents: A1 or A2, S2, S6. A carrier or repair crew needs wages, food, tools, or safety. Negotiate, propose alternatives, or decline. Carry the terms with the workers and equipment.

**W2. A damaged route.** Parents: W1, direct travel, or a local report; S1, S2. A road, relay, or waterwork is unsafe. Survey, reroute, repair, evacuate, or wait. Return with a dated observation and a practical constraint.

**W3. The Silt Papers.** Parents: W2, S3, S7. A survey raises questions about earlier water policy. Read the papers, seek witnesses, compare sources, or do immediate work first. Return with evidence and someone affected by its neglect.

**W4. The Great Weir.** Parents: W3 or a claimant, S1, S2, S4, S6. A person brings a compensation claim or a need for water and power. Examine the promise, negotiate, seek repair, or contest the project. The Weir's benefits and the displacement it caused both remain true.

**W5. The older road.** Parents: W2, S1, S7. A survey touches the route Guest Among Forests helped establish. Compare maps, ask local communities, protect access, or change the work. Her memory does not replace their consent.

#### Names and Sanctuary

**N1. The provisional roll.** Parents: A0 or A4, S3, S7. A household is confirmed, missing, or disputed. Submit a supported entry, mark uncertainty, ask consent, or keep details private. Record who holds the receipt.

**N2. The withdrawn code.** Parents: N1, S3, S4. A Scorch Field or Amber Shore survivor cannot use an old residence code. Seek temporary recognition, correct a register, use witnesses, or protect a private route. An access decision does not reverse the province's physical fall.

**N3. The bones.** Parents: N1 or N2, S3, S7, S8. Bone Script or another petitioner offers names. Bone Script has not disclosed the uncertain reconstructions; trust and events may lead him to choose whether to do so. Verify a subset, protect holders, seek an entry, or disagree about disclosure. Keep shared and private names separate.

**N4. Sanctuary.** Parents: N1 to N3 or any person's immediate need for shelter, S4, S5. Ask the Temple or another refuge, negotiate conditions, or find another route. Record consent, limits, and who knows the location.

**N5. The fires.** Parents: N3 and a credible source, S1, S7. Bone Script heard from a trader that people returned to Nine Willow Bend. Protect the news, seek consent to investigate, compare testimony, or leave the community private. Survival in one place does not prove every Dusk zone safe.

**N6. Overlapping papers.** Parents: N1, N2, or a journey, S3, S4. Stray Stroke's issued papers or another permit help at a checkpoint. Use, verify, conceal, or correct a registration. An issued document can contain a false claim; the harm to Copper Ladle remains an obligation.

#### Authority and Memory

**P1. Reconstruction.** Parents: A4, S3, S4, S7. A claim is affected by a changed register or closing clause. Compare wording, obtain clarification, seek a stay, or contest an interpretation. Record the exact change and who implements it.

**P2. The public account.** Parents: A5 or P1, S5, S6, S7. The Ledger Hand's commemoration reaches people who remember another version. Protect a witness, compare the Year 423 recitation, seek a commitment, or let the rite proceed. Identify the audience and carrier.

**P3. The Charter.** Parents: P1, P2, or a chosen inquiry, S3, S4, S7. Shadow holds a copy. Celestial Book may help trace provenance or another surviving source. Authenticate, protect, publish selectively, bargain, or withhold. Preserve what the accessible evidence establishes.

**P4. Forged edicts.** Parents: P3 or independent evidence, S3, S4, S7. A workshop record, witness, or inscription raises a question. Test provenance, protect workers, challenge a particular order, or seek a hearing. One forged edict does not prove every edict forged.

**P5. The sealed door.** Parents: P3, P4, a scheduled Continuance, or an officiant's request; S1, S5, S7. A rite, witness, or chosen investigation concerns the Spire. Preserve the Continuance, challenge a claim, negotiate access, or seek records elsewhere. Return with observations, consent, and known risks. The Continuance and Full Rite are distinct.

**P6. The wounded conduit.** Parents: P5 and independent evidence, S1, S7. Founding and sealing accounts can be compared with lived experience. Test a theory, investigate another explanation, seek repair, or protect people without resolving it. A proposed sacrifice requires evidence and informed consent; it is not mandatory.

#### Possible Resolutions

**E1. A protected place.** Parents: W, N, or P. Show who may use a route or shelter, who maintains it, and what remains vulnerable.

**E2. A changed institution.** Parents: P1 to P4 or W4. Record a particular clause, allocation, or procedure, its implementation, and any opposition able to act.

**E3. Coalition or separation.** Parents: W1, N4, P2, or P3. Define an agreement and its limits. Groups may cooperate on one matter and disagree on another.

**E4. Departure or succession.** Parents: any chosen branch. Characters may leave, retire, die, or hand work to others. Apply the Core Rulebook's legacy rules through a credible connection. Escape does not automatically end a player character or erase every relationship.

**E5. A wider confrontation.** Parents: established local pressure and the table's choice. Use actual alliances, preparations, evidence, and Dusk conditions. The capital's fall, a final enemy, and a character's sacrifice are possibilities, not prerequisites.

**L4. The western passage.** Parents: S1, S2, S4; W2 or a convoy. A route reaches Sky Pillar, the Silent Monastery, or Iron Gate. Seek an escort, ask about a pass, send a short relay report, or change the journey. Return with the route actually used and the people who traveled it.

**L5. The forest gathering.** Parents: S1, S2, S7; W5 or a community's invitation. A survey reaches Greenroot, the Glimmering Pools, or the Hollow. Listen to local accounts, request access, or withdraw. Return with what people agreed to share and any obligation attached.

**L6. The grassland claim.** Parents: S1, S3, S7; N2 or a herder's request. A Scorch Field family needs grazing, documents, or a way to find relatives. **L7. The coastal memory.** Parents: S1, S3, S7; N2 or a fisher's request. An Amber Shore survivor brings a boat record or an account of the tide. Seek a missing person, preserve a craft, or decide whether to share the record. Return with consent, a source, and an unresolved limit to the evidence.

**L8. The eastern names.** Parents: S1, S3, S7; N3 or an eastern valley community. A water or residence claim touches Nine Willow Bend. Verify names with consent, ask about a survey, or protect information about those who returned. Return with the specific claim and who holds it.

**L9. The steppe account.** Parents: S2, S6, S7; P2 or a herding station's needs. A rider, worker, or family remembers the station where the Iron Brush lived in his youth. Compare their account with the recitation, negotiate for supplies, or let the account remain private. Return with the witness's decision and the material need that brought the parties together.

### Other Networks

The following groups are already in the setting. They are possible participants in the existing nodes, not a second list of required plots. A group enters through a known person, specific action, or credible report; its individual members may disagree.

**H1. Order of the Unheard Prayer.** Parents: S4, S5, S6; P2 or P5. Ritualists question doctrine while carrying out their duties. A protected conversation or a disputed text can lead to testimony, silence, or a request for safety.

**H2. Shattered Chain.** Parents: S2, S4, S6; W1 or F9. Former forced laborers maintain mutual support. A route, wage dispute, or camp record can lead to an escort, a demand, or a refusal to expose members.

**H3. Twelve Pillars.** Parents: S3, S7; P3 or F10. Historians preserve copies in the Warrens. A source can request safe custody or comparison; the party decides what evidence to share.

**H4. Night Garden.** Parents: S1, S5, S7; N4 or P6. Members believe the Dusk transforms rather than merely destroys. Their belief is a position to examine, not proof of its nature; an individual may seek refuge or try to recruit someone.

**H5. Society of the Open Hand.** Parents: S2, S5, S6; A1 or N4. A kitchen or clinic can help a household and raise a question about access to education. A family decides whether to accept an offer for its child.

**H6. Brotherhood of the Silent Step.** Parents: S2, S4; W2 or N6. Couriers share routes and mutual protection. An escort or damaged message can establish the terms of passage and whom the route can safely carry.

**H7. Circle of the Whispering Reed.** Parents: S3, S7; P1 or P3. A copyist preserves a document marked for destruction. The party may arrange custody, compare the copy, or protect its source.

**H8. Guild of the Iron Ledger.** Parents: S2, S3, S6; A2 or W1. Accountants organize for work and protection. A questioned balance or an endangered clerk can lead to a limited exchange, a wage agreement, or a refusal.

The Quiet Company and the Vermilion Trade are further institutional threads. The former connects the Ledger Hand's surveillance mandate to people who practice or remember its breathing discipline. The latter connects Reading Houses and the Counting Rule to P1, P2, and the price paid by an author. Neither is evidence that every School or every official shares one intention.

### Personal Branches

Each branch can begin through a relationship chosen at the table, an existing consequence, or a person acting independently. A named figure's private knowledge is not automatically known by the party. The related career does not impose the portrait's history on a player.

**F1. Cinnabar Heart and the journal.** Parents: A4 or P2, S3, S6, S7. A report asks him to choose what the public will see. **F2. Night Warbler and the missing destination.** Parents: A2, A3, or a chosen relationship; S4, S7. A resettlement manifest names surviving households but lacks their destination. **F3. Guest Among Forests and the old road.** Parents: W2 or W5, S1, S2, S7. A survey follows a route she once showed travelers. Ask the present communities what access and work they will accept, compare her memory with the survey, or choose another route. Return with a local agreement, refusal, or renewed obligation.

**F4. Iron Calculation and the forecast.** Parents: A2, W1, or W4; S2, S4, S6. A repair or allocation requires assumptions about reserves. ### Review Each Node

1. Check parent facts: date, place, rule, and prior decision.
2. Identify what each person wants, knows, can do, and how news reached them.
3. State necessary resources, travel, labor, and time before a choice.
4. Separate testimony, document contents, and interpretation.
5. Offer credible alternatives and apply each established cost once.
6. Bring back a changed person, agreement, object, or place.
7. Compare English and Chinese names and preserve the distinction between an office, a School, and a person.

The setting book, scene seeds, bestiary, and dossiers supply further situations within these systems. A new scene needs an entry and a consequence, not a separate campaign premise. The table may stop at a meaningful result or continue along an established relationship.
