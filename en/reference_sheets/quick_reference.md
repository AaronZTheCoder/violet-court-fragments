# Quick Reference Card

## For the Table

### Modular Careers at a Glance

One primary career, one secondary trade, one specialization. Assign 16, 14, 14, 12, 10, 8, then add six points, maximum 18. Take two primary Skills, one secondary Skill, and two free Skills, five different proficiencies total. Specialization uses a free slot. Only the primary grants abilities, derived bonuses, equipment, and an advancement tree. Start with 10 Celestial Favor, 50 Ritual Harmony, and 20 Copper Leaves. Faction Standing starts at 0; optionally choose +10 with one School and minus 10 with another. Keep named figure histories optional.

### Advantage and Disadvantage

Advantage: Roll 2d20, take higher.
Disadvantage: Roll 2d20, take lower.
They cancel each other.

### Critical

Natural 20: Critical Success. Exceptional result.
Natural 1: Critical Failure. Complication occurs.

### Combat Turn

1. **Action:** Strike, Technique, Guard, Assist, Improvise
2. **Movement:** One Zone (or stand, take cover)
3. **Free Interaction:** Speak, draw weapon, pick up item

### Dying

At 0 HP: Endurance check TN 15 each turn.
Three failures before a success = Death.
Stabilize: Intellect TN 12 with supplies, or healing.

### Zones

- Move one Zone per turn
- Ranged: same Zone or adjacent
- Long range: two Zones (Disadvantage)

### Social Actions

- Persuade: Shift stance better
- Deceive: Lie convincingly
- Probe: Learn target's motive
- Leverage: Force compliance (stance worsens)
- Reveal: Share a truth (automatic +1 stance)

### Ritual Harmony Scale

0 to 20 Heterodox (investigation imminent)
21 to 40 Suspect (no positions, mail opened)
41 to 60 Acceptable (normal, vulnerable in Harmonization Audits)
61 to 80 Harmonious (protected, eligible for advancement)
81 to 100 Exemplary (shield and target)

**Treatment and Recovery:**

- Stabilization: Appropriate supplies and an Intellect check against TN 12 stabilize a Dying character. A healing Technique or item can also do so.
- Medical Treatment: A physician's care costs 5 Silver and heals 1d4 HP while providing continued recovery.
- Sanity Recovery: Meaningful connection restores 1d4 Sanity, and accomplishing a significant goal restores 1d6 Sanity.
- Poison or Disease: Purify Body removes one poison, disease, or minor Dusk corruption, while a Poison Counteragent grants Advantage against ingested poison.

### The Six Schools at a Glance

**Golden Orthodoxy**
Core Belief: The old ways must be preserved. Change is corruption.
Key Figure: Cinnabar Heart
Faction Standing Effect: High Faction Standing protects from Harmonization Audits and investigations. Low Faction Standing attracts scrutiny from Commission investigators.
Typical Members: Historians, traditionalists, conservative officials, elders.
Rival Schools: Bright Mirror (over purity of tradition), Verdant Path (over adaptation).

**Verdant Path**
Core Belief: Heal the land first, and the empire can follow. The empire has never stood apart from nature.
Key Figure: Guest Among Forests
Faction Standing Effect: High Faction Standing grants access to rare medicinal herbs, poisons, and treatments. Low Faction Standing means healers and herbalists refuse service.
Typical Members: Healers, alchemists, farmers, survivalists.
Rival Schools: Golden Orthodoxy (over change), Crimson Lineage (over natural order).

**Iron Calculation**
Core Belief: Everything is a calculation. Efficiency is the highest virtue.
Key Figure: Iron Calculation
Faction Standing Effect: High Faction Standing lowers prices on goods, services, and information. Low Faction Standing triggers economic sanctions and price gouging.
Typical Members: Merchants, accountants, strategists, logisticians.
Rival Schools: Common Flame (over people versus numbers), Crimson Lineage (over noble privilege).

**Crimson Lineage**
Core Belief: Blood remembers what the empire forces itself to forget.
Key Figure: Duke of Eternal Night
Faction Standing Effect: High Faction Standing opens noble courts and high society. Low Faction Standing means barred from social events and noble cooperation.
Typical Members: Nobility, historians of bloodlines, those who study inherited memory.
Rival Schools: Common Flame (over class), Bright Mirror (over purity), Verdant Path (over natural order), Iron Calculation (over noble privilege).

**Common Flame**
Core Belief: Power belongs to the people, not the few.
Key Figure: Iron Bone
Faction Standing Effect: High Faction Standing grants information networks, safe houses, and people's support. Low Faction Standing means whispers turn hostile and crowds become dangerous.
Typical Members: Commoners, laborers, revolutionaries, underground leaders.
Rival Schools: Crimson Lineage (over nobility), Iron Calculation (over exploitation).

**Bright Mirror**
Core Belief: Purity above all. The Dusk is a stain that must be cleansed.
Key Figure: Bright Mirror
Faction Standing Effect: High Faction Standing grants Qi training access and artifact research privileges. Low Faction Standing means marked for purification and investigation.
Typical Members: Qi masters, ascetics, purifiers, investigators.
Rival Schools: Golden Orthodoxy (over what purity means), Crimson Lineage (over blood purity).

**Faction Standing Effects Summary:**

- Revered (80+): School actively protects and promotes the character. Access to elite resources. Personal audience with the Key Figure possible.
- Trusted (50+): School treats character as a valued ally. Discounts, information sharing, minor favors.
- Favored (20+): School views character positively. Basic cooperation. Standard services available.
- Neutral (0): No special treatment. Standard interaction. No hostility but no help.
- Suspect (minus 20): School is wary. Services restricted. Character may be watched.
- Hostile (minus 50): School actively opposes the character. Denied services. Potential for violence.
- Sworn Enemy (minus 80 or below): School dedicates resources to the character's destruction. Assassins. Blacklists. Open hostility.
